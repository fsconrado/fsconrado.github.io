Personal Story

I was born in Joaquim Gomes, in the countryside of Alagoas, but I was raised in Maceió, the city of my heart. My journey is defined by resilience and continuous learning. Despite facing early challenges, including physical disability and the absence of my biological parents, I found a father and unconditional love in God.

I have always been driven by work and art: I sold popsicles at age 12, joined a church band at 14, and by 15, I was already directing and narrating my first school play. It was also during my youth that I met my beloved wife, Rose, the mother of my greatest treasures: Felip (6) and Elenna (1).

In computing, I discovered a transformative power. Technology didn't just present me with a new horizon; it drastically reduced the physical barriers imposed by a shortening in my left leg, proving that knowledge knows no bounds.

Today, my mission is clear: I strive to continuously evolve and contribute so that technology makes the world a more accessible and human place for everyone.
